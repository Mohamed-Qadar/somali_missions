# Citizen Guide

## Finding a mission

Go to the homepage or **Directory** to search by country, mission type (embassy/consulate), or rating. Each mission page shows contact details, opening hours, a document pre-check for common services, and a map.

## Before you travel to a mission

Visit **Before You Travel**, pick your mission and the service you need (e.g. passport renewal), and see the exact document checklist and typical processing time before you go - this avoids wasted trips.

## Booking an appointment

1. Go to **Book Appointment**, choose your mission and service.
2. Pick an available time slot. If the slot is full, you're automatically placed on a waitlist instead of being turned away.
3. You'll get a confirmation page with a QR code and (if you gave an email) a confirmation email. Show the QR code at the mission on the day.
4. Staff may approve, reject, or reschedule your request - you'll get an email and, if you're logged in, an in-app notification (bell icon, top right) when your status changes.

## Joining the walk-in queue

If you're already at the mission without an appointment, use **Join the Queue** to get a ticket number and an estimated wait time based on how fast the mission is actually serving people that day. The mission's own display screen shows "now serving."

## Submitting a complaint

Use **Submit Complaint**. You can submit anonymously (no email/phone required) or with your contact details. Either way you get an **8-character tracking code** - save it, it's the only way to check your complaint's status later (there's no account requirement).

If it's a genuine emergency (stranded, urgent safety issue), use **Emergency Support** instead - it skips the normal queue and alerts a duty officer directly.

## Tracking your complaint

Go to **Track Complaint** and enter your tracking code. You'll see the current status, timeline, and any official response. If a secure message thread is open on your case, you can also reply there using the same code.

## Asking the assistant

**Ask the Assistant** answers common questions (lost passport, visa, birth certificate, office hours, etc.) instantly and suggests what to do next (book an appointment, submit a complaint, or view document requirements).

## Creating an account

An account isn't required to submit or track a complaint, but registering lets you see all your complaints/appointments in one dashboard and get in-app notifications. After registering, check your email (or, in a local dev setup with no real email configured, the "simulate" link shown on screen) to verify your address.
